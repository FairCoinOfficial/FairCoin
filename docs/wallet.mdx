# FAIRWallet

**FAIRWallet** is the self-custody wallet for native FAIR. You hold your own keys — there is no account, no custodian, and no KYC to send or receive.

## What you can do

- **Send & receive** native FAIR using `F`-prefixed addresses.
- **FastSend** — guaranteed near-instant, spendable-before-confirmation transfers, ideal at a point of sale.
- **Stake** — keep the wallet open and online to earn [staking](/developers/docs/faircoin/staking) rewards on your balance.
- **Manage masternodes** — start and monitor a [masternode](/developers/docs/faircoin/masternodes) from the Masternodes tab (desktop).

## Get it

Download from [fairco.in/wallet](https://fairco.in/wallet) for your platform. On desktop, the official **FairCoin Core** wallet (the Qt GUI built from [source](/developers/docs/faircoin/run-a-node)) also gives you the full node, debug console, and masternode controls.

## Back up your wallet

Your coins live on the blockchain; your **keys** live in the wallet. If you lose the keys, the coins are gone.

1. Write down your recovery phrase (mobile) the moment you create the wallet, and store it offline.
2. On desktop, back up `wallet.dat` and keep a copy somewhere safe. Re-back-up after creating new addresses.
3. Never share your recovery phrase or private keys with anyone — no support agent will ever ask for them.
