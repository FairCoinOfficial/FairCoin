# WFAIR bridge

The **WFAIR bridge** issues *Wrapped FairCoin* (`WFAIR`) — an ERC-20 token on **Base** that is backed 1:1 by native FAIR. It's how FairCoin reaches the EVM world: USDC on-ramps, DeFi, and instant transfers, all redeemable for the real coin.

## How it works

The bridge is a custodial mint/burn vault. Native FAIR locked in the reserve is matched 1:1 by WFAIR in circulation, so the wrapped supply can never exceed the FAIR backing it.

**Wrap (FAIR → WFAIR)**
1. Send native FAIR to the bridge deposit address.
2. The bridge confirms the deposit and mints the same amount of WFAIR to your Base address.

**Unwrap (WFAIR → FAIR)**
1. Submit your WFAIR to the bridge on Base (via [/unwrap](https://fairco.in/unwrap)).
2. The bridge burns the WFAIR and releases the equivalent native FAIR to your `F` address.

For the user-facing flow, see [Get FAIR](/developers/docs/faircoin/get-fair).

## Reserves & transparency

Because backing is 1:1, the WFAIR total supply on Base should always equal (or be less than) the FAIR held in the bridge reserve. Both sides are publicly auditable:

- **WFAIR supply** — read `totalSupply()` on the [token contract](https://basescan.org/address/0xF2853CedDF47A05Fee0B4b24DFf2925d59737fb3).
- **FAIR reserve** — inspect the reserve address on the [FairCoin explorer](https://explorer.fairco.in).

## For developers

WFAIR is a standard ERC-20, so it works with any Base tooling out of the box (wagmi/viem, ethers, Uniswap, etc.). Read live bridge stats — supply, reserves, status — from the contract, or wire the [bridge source](https://github.com/FairCoinOfficial/faircoin-bridge) into your own integration.
