# FairCoin

**FairCoin** is a community-run cryptocurrency built for everyday payments — the kind a local shop can actually accept. It exists in two interoperable forms:

- **FAIR** — the native coin on the FairCoin blockchain. A PoW/PoS hybrid network secured by miners, stakers, and masternodes.
- **WFAIR** — *Wrapped FairCoin*, an ERC-20 token on **Base** backed 1:1 by native FAIR. It lets you buy, hold, and move FairCoin with everyday DeFi and USDC rails.

## Why two forms?

Native FAIR is great for self-custody, staking, and masternodes. But onboarding new users to a standalone chain is hard. **WFAIR** removes that friction: anyone with a Base wallet and USDC can hold FairCoin in seconds, then **unwrap** to native FAIR whenever they want the full on-chain experience. The [bridge](/developers/docs/faircoin/bridge) keeps the two in 1:1 lockstep.
